About these observations

The introducer queues advertised endpoints for background verification and retries failures before sharing them. Verified peers have completed a successful Chia handshake within the configured lifetime; this does not guarantee they are currently online or synced. Known peers describe the discovery backend; only peers with an unexpired introducer verification are advertised.

Activity totals reset when the service restarts. Individual endpoints are not published on this dashboard. JSON statistics · Prometheus metrics
